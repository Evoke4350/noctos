noctos is an open-source Flutter app implementing the full 6-week CBT-I (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia) protocol. CBT-I is the first-line clinical recommendation for chronic insomnia and outperforms hypnotics long-term.

What it does:
- Tracks a structured sleep diary (bedtime, lights-out, awakenings, WASO, wake, quality, mood, adherence)
- Computes a personalized sleep-restriction prescription weekly based on rolling efficiency
- Walks you through assessment, sleep restriction, stimulus control, cognitive techniques, hygiene, and relapse prevention
- Logs caffeine with a half-life decay curve and predicted dose at bedtime
- Worry journal with a bedtime-window guardrail
- Daily reminders: wind-down, bedtime, wake, caffeine cutoff
- Chronotype quiz (MEQ short form)

What it does NOT do:
- No sleep score, streaks, or gamification (orthosomnia is real harm)
- No accounts, no cloud sync, no telemetry
- Not a replacement for clinical care for severe insomnia

All data stays on your device in encrypted SQLite. Export to JSON or CSV anytime.
